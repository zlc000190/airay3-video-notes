# AI Ray 3 decision and exception log

[AI Ray 3](https://airay3.video/) is the project entry point for this independent AI video workflow review template. Verify current availability, behavior, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Make judgment traceable

Log each material choice with the date, decision owner, evidence reviewed, alternatives considered, selected option, expected consequence, review date, and reversal trigger. Include licensed source media, saved prompts, render settings, temporal checks, and usage-rights evidence.

## Exception record

For any deviation from the normal rule, capture:

1. the exact control or requirement being waived;
2. the bounded scope and expiry time;
3. the evidence that made the exception necessary;
4. compensating checks and an accountable approver;
5. the rollback or closure condition;
6. proof that the exception was removed or renewed after review.

## Review gate

Do not use an exception to bypass privacy, identity verification, licensing, payment authorization, or platform security controls. An undocumented verbal approval is not durable evidence. Close stale exceptions and re-test affected outputs after the normal rule is restored.
