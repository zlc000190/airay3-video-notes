# AI Ray 3 decommission and data-deletion checklist

[AI Ray 3](https://airay3.video/) is the project entry point for this independent AI video workflow review template. Verify current availability, behavior, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Retirement inventory

List every active endpoint, deployment, repository, scheduled job, integration, credential, datastore, backup, billing item, redirect, and public document associated with the reviewed workflow. Reconcile the inventory with licensed source media, saved prompts, render settings, temporal checks, and usage-rights evidence.

## Controlled shutdown

1. Export only the authorized records that must be retained and validate readability.
2. Notify affected owners and publish a clear replacement or end-of-life path.
3. Disable writes, jobs, webhooks, and automated actions before revoking access.
4. Revoke credentials, remove integrations, stop billing, and archive required evidence.
5. Delete data from primary storage, caches, queues, logs, and backups under the applicable policy.
6. Verify redirects, error behavior, search-visible pages, and downstream references.
7. Record deletion evidence, exceptions, retention deadlines, and final approver.

## Closure gate

Do not claim deletion from a successful API response alone. Confirm the data is no longer retrievable through normal access paths and that retained copies have a lawful, time-bounded reason. Never place secrets, personal data, or identity documents in the closure report.
