# AI Ray 3 maintenance ownership checklist

[AI Ray 3](https://airay3.video/) is the project entry point for this independent AI video workflow review template. Confirm current availability, features, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Named responsibilities

Assign an owner and backup for:

- primary-source and policy refresh;
- test fixtures and expected results;
- credentials, permissions, and rotation;
- monitoring, incident triage, and user notices;
- backups, restore rehearsals, and retirement;
- accessibility, privacy, licensing, and jurisdiction review.

The maintenance evidence should cover licensed media, prompt records, temporal-consistency checks, and usage-rights review.

## Review cadence

Use both calendar and event triggers. Recheck after a material product update, source-policy change, new data category, pricing change, incident, ownership transfer, or failed known-answer test.

## Handoff gate

A handoff is incomplete without current repository and deployment locations, open risks, last successful verification, next scheduled review, rollback instructions, and a contact path that belongs to the organization rather than a private individual.

Retire stale guidance explicitly. Preserve an audit record of what changed and why, while removing secrets and personal data from operational documentation.
