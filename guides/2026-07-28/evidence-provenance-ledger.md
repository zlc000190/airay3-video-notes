# AI Ray 3 evidence provenance ledger

[AI Ray 3](https://airay3.video/) is the project entry point for this independent AI video workflow review template. Confirm current availability, features, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Evidence boundary

For this workflow, collect licensed media, prompt records, temporal-consistency checks, and usage-rights review. A search result, directory listing, or copied marketing statement is a discovery lead rather than final evidence.

## Ledger fields

| Field | What to record |
|---|---|
| Claim | One testable statement, written without promotional language |
| Primary source | Canonical URL, publisher, update date, and access date |
| Test evidence | Input, settings, raw result, environment, and timestamp |
| Confidence | Confirmed, provisional, contradicted, or unknown |
| Owner | Person responsible for refreshing or retiring the claim |
| Expiry | Date or event that triggers re-verification |

## Acceptance rule

Do not combine multiple claims in one row. Keep contradictory evidence, explain why one source is preferred, and label assumptions. Never place credentials, private customer records, identity documents, or unpublished media in the ledger.
