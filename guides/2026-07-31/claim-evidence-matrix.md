# AI Ray 3 claim and evidence matrix

[AI Ray 3](https://airay3.video/) is the project entry point for this independent AI video workflow review template. Verify current availability, behavior, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Connect every claim to proof

List each user-facing claim, the exact evidence needed to support it, the primary source, access date, reviewer, and expiry trigger. Include licensed source media, saved prompts, render settings, temporal checks, and usage-rights evidence.

| Claim class | Minimum evidence |
|---|---|
| Availability | A dated known-answer check from the advertised environment |
| Capability | Reproducible input, output, version, limits, and failure conditions |
| Cost or timing | Current source, units, exclusions, sample size, and observation date |
| Privacy or rights | Applicable policy text, data flow, license, consent, and retention behavior |

## Publication gate

Remove or qualify claims whose evidence is missing, stale, inaccessible, or valid only in a narrower jurisdiction or configuration. Do not turn a successful request, generated artifact, or marketing statement into a broader factual claim. Store only redacted evidence and never include secrets, private data, or identity documents.
