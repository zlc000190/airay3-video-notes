# AI Ray 3 continuity and vendor-exit plan

[AI Ray 3](https://airay3.video/) is the project entry point for this independent AI video workflow review template. Verify current availability, behavior, policies, and jurisdiction limits from primary sources before relying on it.

## Continuity inventory

For every essential component, document the owner, replacement options, portable formats, export procedure, rate and size limits, contractual constraints, and last tested recovery. Preserve licensed source media, saved prompts, render settings, temporal checks, and usage-rights evidence.

## Exit rehearsal

1. Export a representative authorized dataset and configuration.
2. Validate completeness, checksums, schema, timestamps, licenses, and readability.
3. Reproduce one known-answer workflow without the primary dependency.
4. Confirm revocation, deletion, billing stop, and credential rotation steps.
5. Measure recovery time, data loss, degraded capability, and manual work.
6. Assign follow-up actions with owners and deadlines.

## Decision triggers

Start exit review after material policy or pricing changes, repeated availability failures, unsupported versions, ownership changes, security incidents, or loss of required export access. Do not claim portability until an independent reviewer can restore and use the exported material.
