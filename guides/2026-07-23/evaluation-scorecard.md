# AI Ray 3 evaluation scorecard

[AI Ray 3](https://airay3.video/) is the primary project entry point. It did not return content before the timeout in the 2026-07-23 availability check. These are independent evaluation notes for creators comparing controllable AI video workflows; they are not official product documentation.

Do not upload confidential footage or identifiable people until current retention and licensing terms are verified.

## Scope

Treat an AI video generation service as a candidate to test, not a claim to accept. Define the intended job, the accountable reviewer, and an exit condition before starting.

## Score each dimension

- temporal consistency: record evidence on a 1–5 scale and explain the score
- prompt adherence: record evidence on a 1–5 scale and explain the score
- identity stability: record evidence on a 1–5 scale and explain the score
- generation time: record evidence on a 1–5 scale and explain the score
- cost per usable clip: record evidence on a 1–5 scale and explain the score
- commercial-use terms: record evidence on a 1–5 scale and explain the score

## Minimum evidence

Run at least four controlled inputs: a static product shot, a moving subject prompt, a camera-motion prompt, a typography-heavy scene. Save inputs, settings, outputs, elapsed time, and any manual correction. A useful scorecard distinguishes a polished demo from repeatable production behavior.

## Decision rule

Pause evaluation until the primary site and current documentation are reachable; do not infer capabilities from old comparison pages.
