# AI Ray 3 benchmark protocol

[AI Ray 3](https://airay3.video/) is the primary project entry point. It did not return content before the timeout in the 2026-07-23 availability check. These are independent evaluation notes for creators comparing controllable AI video workflows; they are not official product documentation.

Do not upload confidential footage or identifiable people until current retention and licensing terms are verified.

## Prepare the run

Use a clean browser profile or disposable environment. Record the date, current product version when visible, account tier, region, and every non-default setting. Use only synthetic or public test material.

## Test set

- Scenario 1: a static product shot
- Scenario 2: a moving subject prompt
- Scenario 3: a camera-motion prompt
- Scenario 4: a typography-heavy scene

Repeat each scenario once without changing inputs. Compare not only the best output, but also variance, latency, errors, and the amount of human repair required.

## Capture results

For each run, log temporal consistency, prompt adherence, identity stability, generation time, cost per usable clip, commercial-use terms. Keep screenshots or exported artifacts where permitted, and separate observed behavior from expectations copied from marketing material.

## Pass condition

A pass requires reproducible evidence for the intended workflow and no unresolved high-impact failure. Pause evaluation until the primary site and current documentation are reachable; do not infer capabilities from old comparison pages.
